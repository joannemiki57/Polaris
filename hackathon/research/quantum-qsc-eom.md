# Quantum Brief: q-sc-EOM Excited-State Chemistry

## Paper
- Title: Molecular Excited States using Quantum Subspace Methods: Accuracy, Resource Reduction, and Error-Mitigated Hardware Implementation of q-sc-EOM
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05380
- Domain: Quantum chemistry, excited-state simulation

## Condensed Abstract Insight
The work combines advanced ground/excited-state methods and targets practical quantum utility for excited-state chemistry. It reports major measurement-scaling reductions, applies error mitigation on hardware, and identifies gate noise as the dominant remaining challenge while achieving encouraging excited-state estimates.

## Problem The Paper Tries To Solve
- Excited-state molecular simulation is computationally hard and high-value.
- Near-term hardware constraints make accurate chemistry difficult.
- Measurement scaling and noise are major blockers.

## Research Friction Exposed
- Resource scaling quickly explodes for realistic molecules.
- Error-mitigation stacks are complex and fragile.
- Translating algorithmic gains into industrial chemistry workflows is nontrivial.
